# EPIC-0007: Automated LRCLIB Synchronized Lyrics Integration

## Summary
Integrates LRCLIB (`https://lrclib.net/`) into Flexioke to automatically discover and synchronize timestamped `.lrc` lyrics during background stem separation and provide a 1-click on-demand lyric auto-fetch action inside the Song Details & Lyrics editor modal.
